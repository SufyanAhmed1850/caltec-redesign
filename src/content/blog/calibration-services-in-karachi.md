Karachi is where much of Pakistan's pharmaceutical, textile, food and energy production happens. In those plants, measurement accuracy isn't a nice-to-have; it's the basis of product release, safety sign-off and every external audit.

If you have never worked with us before, here is what to expect.

## You choose where the work happens

Some instruments can't leave the line. Others are easier to bring to us. We run both models: mobile teams arrive with certified reference standards, and our Korangi workshop handles bench calibration and repair.

## Scope comes before promises

We ask for your instrument list, ranges, locations and deadline first. That way the quotation reflects the real job, and nothing surprises your team on the day.

## Procedures you can defend

Pressure, temperature, flow, humidity, electrical, mechanical and dimensional work all follow documented methods. Certificates record as-found and as-left data, the reference used and the conditions at the time.

## People who explain the result

A certificate is only useful if your team understands it. Our technicians walk through what they found, what was adjusted and what to watch before the next interval.

## What makes the difference

- **Cross-industry experience** in pharma, food, textiles, cement and energy
- **ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001** certified management systems
- **Planned maintenance** schedules so instruments never slip out of date
- **Audit readiness** for cleanrooms, compressors and other critical systems

From Korangi to SITE, CALTEC supports QA managers, plant engineers and compliance teams who treat precision as part of their strategy. [Start an enquiry](/enquiry/) and we'll help you define the right scope.
